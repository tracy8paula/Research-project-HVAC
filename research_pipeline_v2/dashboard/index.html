How this prototype works
  1. Recorded two-stream Building 59 observations are replayed as incoming sensor messages.
  2. The dashboard maintains a rolling history of 12 ten-minute samples (120 minutes).
  3. The validation-selected aggregate LSTM predicts CO₂ and temperature at 10, 20 and 30 minutes ahead.
  4. When the replay reaches the 30-minute target timestamp, the stored prediction is compared with the recorded observation and running MAE is updated.
  5. Historical model, synthetic scenario and control outputs are displayed in separate tabs.
The model used for live replay is the aggregate LSTM checkpoint selected using validation performance. It is not presented as the best model on the historical test comparison.
Current experiment definition: 10-minute sampling, 120-minute input history, 30-minute forecast horizon. Historical test outputs are exploratory because the test partition was inspected before a later validation-objective correction. Uganda scenarios are synthetic. Spatial coordinates are virtual assumptions. No physical sensor connection or calibrated HVAC energy measurement is included.
